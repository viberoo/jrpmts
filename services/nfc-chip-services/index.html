NFC Marketing Pro Tips

Make every tap count

Focus on a Single Objective

Don't overwhelm the user. Link your tag to one optimized landing page with a singular goal—whether that's booking an estimate, following a social profile, or viewing a digital menu.

Contextual Call-to-Actions (CTAs)

An NFC chip is invisible tech; your signage shouldn't be. If a tag is embedded in a poster or display, tell the user why they should tap.

Measure, Test, and Optimize

If you can't measure it, you can't improve it. Pair your NFC links with analytics (like UTM parameters or specialized NFC platforms) to track tap volume and conversion rate.

Future-Proof with Dynamic Links

Never link directly to a static file. Use dynamic URLs that allow you to update the destination link on the backend without ever having to replace the physical NFC hardware. This saves money and allows for seasonal promotions.

Strategic Placement

Place tags where customers naturally dwell or seek information.
